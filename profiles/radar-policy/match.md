# Match

Classify policies, standards, procurement, tenders and contract awards only when the source establishes a direct connection to a telecom operator and its AI models, compute, GPUs, data centers or related AI infrastructure. Operator groups, subsidiaries, carrier cloud businesses and government rules explicitly applicable to telecom operators qualify.

A general AI policy, vendor announcement, government project or procurement is not enough merely because operators could potentially be affected. Do not infer operator involvement. Exclude unrelated routine operator purchasing, earnings and stock-market performance. If operator relevance is unclear, prefer another appropriate profile.
