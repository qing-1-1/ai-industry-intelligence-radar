# Evaluation goal

AI model routing, LLM gateway, provider routing, model fallback, model selection and inference cost. Exclude traditional network routers, switches and 5G hardware unless directly about AI model routing.

# Scoring rubric

9-10: material official strategy, financial results, pricing or major release. 7-8: significant concrete development. 5-6: relevant incremental news. 3-4: weak relevance or promotion. 0-2: unrelated or unsupported. Prioritize primary sources. Preserve reporting period, currency, units and metric; never equate total AI revenue with token revenue. Score irrelevant stories below 3. Supply 3-5 factual tags including companies where confirmed.
