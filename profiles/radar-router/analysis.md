# Evaluation goal

AI model router, LLM gateway, provider routing, intelligent selection, failover and cost/latency optimization relevant to telecom operator or enterprise model services. Exclude conventional routers, switches and 5G routers.

# Scoring rubric

9-10: official, material operator AI product, price, capacity, major tender or policy with concrete verified details. 7-8: significant relevant operational development. 6: specific useful industry signal. 4-5: weak or indirect relevance. 0-3: unrelated or promotional. Standalone earnings and stock-price news must score below 4. Prefer operator relevance and authoritative primary sources. Do not infer quantities, award status, currency, conditions or dates. Use 3-5 factual tags and identify organizations only when supported.
